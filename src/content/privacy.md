## What we collect to care for you

When you book and see Dr Sam, we collect:

- **Who you are:** your name, date of birth, address, phone, email and emergency contact.
- **Your health information:** your pre-assessment answers, the observer form, your history, test results, appointment notes and your written plan.
- **Medicare details:** only if you're seen in person or a Medicare rebate applies.
- **Payment details:** handled by the booking platform. [TODO: confirm we never see full card numbers]
- **Messages:** phone messages and emails you send us.

We collect it from you, from the person who fills in the observer form, and, with your permission, from other doctors and test providers.

If you don't give us this information, we can't safely assess you or provide care.

## The observer form

Someone who knew you growing up, like a parent or partner, fills in a short form about you. You give us their name and email, and they choose whether to take part.

We use what they tell us only for your assessment. It becomes part of your health record.

## When we share your information

We share your information only:

- with the services listed above, so they can do their job
- with your GP or other health professionals involved in your care, with your permission
- with pathology or ECG providers, if you need a test
- when the law requires or allows it, for example to prevent a serious threat to someone's life or safety

We never sell your information. We won't use it for marketing without your permission.

## Staying anonymous

You can ask general questions without giving your name, for example by phone. To be assessed or treated, we need to know who you are.
