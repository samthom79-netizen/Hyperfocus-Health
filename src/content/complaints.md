## Tell us first

Most problems are fixed fastest by talking to us. Email {contact.email}.

It helps to include:

- your name and how to reach you
- what happened, and when
- what you'd like us to do

We reply within {contact.complaintsReplyWithin}. Making a complaint won't affect your care.

## If you're still not happy

### The Office of the Health Ombudsman (OHO)

The OHO is Queensland's independent health complaints agency. It handles complaints about health services and health practitioners.

- Phone: {legal.ohoPhone}, {legal.ohoHours}
- Online: [make a complaint to the OHO]({links.ohoComplaint})

In Queensland, concerns about a registered practitioner's conduct also go to the OHO. It considers them together with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency ([Ahpra]({links.ahpra})).

### Privacy complaints

If your complaint is about your personal information, see our [privacy policy](/privacy). If we haven't replied after 30 days, or you're unhappy with our answer, you can go to the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner]({links.oaicComplaint}) on {legal.oaicPhone}.
