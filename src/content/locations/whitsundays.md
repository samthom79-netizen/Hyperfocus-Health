## In person at Cannonvale, or by video or phone

Dr Sam lives in the Whitsundays and works at Cannonvale Medical Centre. You can see him there in person, or by video or phone from anywhere private in Queensland.

In-person appointments are booked through the clinic. The fees are the same as video or phone, and you may be able to claim a Medicare rebate. After an in-person visit, later video and phone appointments may attract a rebate too.

## If you'll see Dr Sam as your regular GP

If you're going to keep seeing Dr Sam at Cannonvale as your regular GP, you may be eligible for a GP chronic condition management plan. If so, he can do it during appointment 2.

The plan can give you Medicare rebates for some visits to a psychologist, dietitian or other allied health worker. See [prices](/pricing) for how this works.

## Working in tourism or hospitality

Early starts, late finishes and boat days are common here. The online intake can be done in short blocks, whenever you have a few minutes.

For appointment 1 (about 60 min), choose a time when you won't be rushed.

## Baseline checks

Before your first appointment, Dr Sam needs your blood pressure, pulse and weight. If you're seeing him in person, these can be done at the clinic.

If your history means you need blood tests or an ECG, Dr Sam will tell you after reading your intake. You can have them at a pathology collection centre or through your GP.
