## Video from the coast or the hinterland

You can join from anywhere private in Queensland. What matters most is a connection that holds for about an hour.

If your internet at home is patchy, try a short video call with a friend in the same spot first. A phone on mobile data, close to a window, sometimes works better than home wi-fi.

## Already seeing someone on the Coast?

If you already have a GP, psychologist or counsellor on the Sunshine Coast, keep them. With your permission, Dr Sam shares your written plan, so you don't have to explain everything twice.

Once things are stable, you can also move your ongoing care to your own Sunshine Coast GP, if they agree in writing to take over.
