## Living near the border

This service is only for people who live in Queensland and are in Queensland for their appointments. If you live in Tweed Heads or elsewhere in New South Wales, we can't see you, and your own GP is the right place to start.

The same applies if you live in Queensland but cross the border for work. Join each appointment from the Queensland side.

## Queensland time

Queensland doesn't use daylight saving, but New South Wales does. In summer the two sides of the border are an hour apart.

Your booking confirmation shows Queensland time. If your phone has switched to New South Wales time, double-check before you join.
