## Video, phone or in person

You can choose video or phone, from home or anywhere private in Queensland. If you'd rather meet face to face, Dr Sam also sees people at Cannonvale Medical Centre in the Whitsundays.

In-person appointments are booked through the clinic. The fees are the same as video or phone, and you may be able to claim a Medicare rebate. After an in-person visit, later video and phone appointments may attract a rebate too.

## If you work a roster

Shift and fly-in fly-out work is common around Mackay and the Bowen Basin. The online intake can be done in short blocks, whenever you have a few minutes.

For appointment 1 (about 60 min), pick a day off if you can. A private room at camp works, as long as no one can overhear.
