## Video or phone, from Far North Queensland

You can join from anywhere private in Queensland. What matters most is a connection that holds for about an hour.

In the wet season, storms can knock out power or internet. Charge your phone beforehand. If the internet drops, a phone appointment still works.

## Remote communities

If you live in a remote community, including Cape York or the Torres Strait, your local health centre may be able to help with blood pressure, pulse and weight checks.

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people can also call 13YARN on 13 92 76, any time, for crisis support from an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander person.
