## How appointments work from Brisbane

You don't travel anywhere. Your appointments happen by video or phone, whichever you prefer, wherever you have privacy.

If you work in the city, a booked meeting room or your parked car can work for a short review. For the first appointment (about 60 min), home is usually easier.

## Studying or starting out

If you're at university or TAFE in Brisbane, the online intake fits around classes. Do it in short blocks, by voice, tick boxes or typing.

Your campus health service or your own GP can usually help with the blood pressure, pulse and weight checks before appointment 1.

## Already seeing a psychologist?

Many people in Brisbane already have a psychologist or counsellor. With your permission, Dr Sam can share your written plan with them, so everyone works from the same page.
