    time: "he emails you"
    you: "Read his email. It's one of three things: an invitation to book appointment 1, a request for something else first, or an explanation if this service isn't right for you."
    doctor: "Reads your answers and the observer form, and tells you plainly what happens next."
  - title: "Baseline checks"
    time: "short; tests only if needed"
    you: "Get your blood pressure, pulse and weight checked, usually by your own GP or a pharmacy. Have blood tests or an ECG only if Dr Sam asks for them."

    time: "about 60 min"
    you: "Book it when invited and pay {prices.payAppointment1}. Talk about your history and what day-to-day life is like."
    doctor: "Goes through your pre-assessment form with you and assesses for ADHD."

    you: "Book it and pay {prices.payAppointment2} ({prices.assessmentTotal} in total). Hear what Dr Sam found and ask anything you like."

    time: "every 2-4 weeks"
    you: "Fill in a short check-in form before each review."

    time: "every 3-6 months once stable"
    you: "Keep seeing Dr Sam, or move to a GP of your choice who agrees in writing to take over."
