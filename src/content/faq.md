groups:
  - heading: "Before you book"
    items:
      - question: "Do I need a referral?"
        answer: "No. You can book directly."
      - question: "Can a GP diagnose ADHD in Queensland?"
        answer: "Yes. In Queensland, specialist GPs can assess, diagnose and manage ADHD in adults, though not every GP chooses to. See the [Queensland Government information](https://www.qld.gov.au/health/conditions/all/adhd). If your situation needs a psychiatrist, Dr Sam will tell you."
      - question: "I live outside Queensland. Can I use this service?"
        answer: "Not at this stage. This service is only for adults who live in Queensland and will be in Queensland for their appointments. Your own GP is the right place to start."

  - heading: "Cost"
    items:
      - question: "Why is there no Medicare rebate for telehealth?"
        answer: "Medicare only pays for a GP video appointment if you've had a face-to-face visit with the same practice in the last 12 months. As a new patient of a video service, you won't have. In person at Cannonvale, the fees are the same and you may be able to claim a rebate. [See all prices](/pricing)."

  - heading: "The assessment"
    items:
      - question: "Who fills in the observer form, and what if I don't have anyone?"
        answer: "Someone who knew you growing up, like a parent, or someone who knows you well now, like a partner. If you don't have anyone, say so in your intake. Dr Sam will talk with you about other ways to fill in the picture, such as old school reports."
      - question: "Can I do the intake in several sittings, or by voice?"
        answer: "Yes. Do it all at once or in short blocks, and answer by voice, tick boxes or typing. It takes about 30 minutes in total."
      - question: "What do I need for a video appointment?"
        answer: "A phone or laptop with a camera, a quiet room where you can talk privately, and photo ID. [See the full checklist](/how-it-works#what-you-need)."

  - heading: "After diagnosis"
    items:
      - question: "What happens if I am diagnosed?"
        answer: "You leave appointment 2 with a written plan. Dr Sam talks you through all your options, including strategies that don't involve medicine. Any treatment is a decision you make together. Reviews follow every 2-4 weeks while things are adjusted."
      - question: "What if I'm not diagnosed with ADHD?"
        answer: "Dr Sam explains what he found and what could help next. {policies.notDiagnosed}"
      - question: "Can I move my ongoing care to my own GP later?"
        answer: "Yes. Once things are stable, you can keep seeing Dr Sam every 3-6 months, or move to a GP of your choice who agrees in writing to take over. With your permission, Dr Sam sends them a summary."

  - heading: "Privacy and support"
    items:
      - question: "Where is my information stored?"
        answer: "This website doesn't collect your health information. The 60-second check stays on your device. {privacy.storage}"
      - question: "What if I'm struggling right now?"
        answer: "If you're in danger now, call 000. You can also call Lifeline on {crisis.lines.0.number} or 1300 MH CALL on {crisis.lines.1.number}, any time of day or night. More support numbers are at the bottom of this page."
