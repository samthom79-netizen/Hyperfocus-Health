This service is designed for adults whose ADHD isn't complicated by other health conditions. Some conditions need a doctor who can see the whole picture, often in person.

That isn't a judgement about you. It's about making sure you get care that's safe and complete.

## Why some people should start elsewhere

- **Some conditions can look like ADHD.** Sorting out which is which can take more than video appointments.
- **Some health histories need closer monitoring.** Heart, mood and substance-use histories are examples.
- **Your own GP knows you.** They can bring your other care together in one place.

## Who to see

- **Your regular GP.** A good first step. They can assess you, help with other conditions and refer you on if needed.
- **A psychiatrist.** A specialist in mental health. You'll need a referral from your GP to get a Medicare rebate.
- **If you're struggling right now,** see the support numbers at the bottom of this page. In an emergency, call 000.

## How to ask your GP for a referral

1. **Book a longer appointment.** When you book, say it's about ADHD so they allow enough time.
2. **Bring a few notes.** Write down what you notice day to day, at work and at home. Bring old school reports if you have them.
3. **Say what you'd like.** For example: "I'd like to be assessed for ADHD. Could you assess me, or refer me to someone who can?"
4. **Ask about next steps.** Check the likely costs, and what happens while you wait.
