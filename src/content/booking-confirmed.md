1. **Check your email and texts.** Your confirmation comes first, then a link to the online intake.
2. **Do the online intake on your phone.** About 30 minutes. Do it all at once or in short blocks. Answer by voice, tick boxes or typing.
3. **Ask someone who knew you growing up to fill in a short form.** A partner or parent is ideal. They get their own link.
4. **Do your baseline checks.** Blood pressure, pulse and weight. Blood tests or an ECG only if your history needs them. Your intake explains how.
5. **Look out for reminders.** You'll get a reminder before each video appointment.
