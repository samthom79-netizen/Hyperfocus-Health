1. **Check your email and texts.** Your confirmation comes first, then a link to the online intake.
2. **Do the online intake on your phone.** About 30 minutes. Do it all at once or in short blocks. Answer by voice, tick boxes or typing.
3. **Ask someone who knew you growing up to fill in a short form.** A partner or parent is ideal. They get their own link.
4. **Wait for Dr Sam's email.** It will be one of three things: an invitation to book appointment 1, a request for something else first (such as your blood pressure, or a blood test or ECG if your history needs one), or an explanation if this service isn't right for you, with where to go instead.
5. **Book appointment 1 when invited.** That's when the next {prices.payAppointment1} is paid. You'll get a reminder before each appointment.
