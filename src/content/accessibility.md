## Our target

We aim for every page to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2]({links.wcag}) at level AA. That's the standard most Australian organisations work to.

## What we've done

- **Easy to read:** body text is at least 18px, lines are short, and we write in plain English.
- **Easy to scan:** a "Short version" box sits at the top of long pages, and each step shows how long it takes.
- **Colour:** every text and background pair is tested for contrast.
- **Keyboard:** everything works without a mouse, with a clear focus outline.
- **Touch:** buttons and links are at least 44px tall.
- **Motion:** nothing plays by itself, and we respect your device's reduce-motion setting.
- **Works without extras:** pages show their full text even with JavaScript turned off.
