## General information, not medical advice

Everything on this website is general information. It isn't advice about your own health.

Reading it, or doing the 60-second check, doesn't make you a patient. You become a patient once you book and Dr Sam accepts you after reading your intake.

**In an emergency, call 000.** For crisis support any time, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 or 1300 MH CALL on 1300 642 255.

## Who the service is for

Adults 18 and over who live in Queensland and will be in Queensland for each appointment. Please don't book if this isn't you.

The 60-second check is a guide only. Dr Sam decides whether the service is safe and suitable for you after reading your intake.

## Booking, fees and cancellations

- Changes, cancellations, missed appointments and refunds follow our [cancellations and refunds](/cancellations) page.
- The fee pays for Dr Sam's time and assessment, not a particular result.
